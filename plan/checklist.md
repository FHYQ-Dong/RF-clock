# RF-clock checklist

This is the reference version; `plan/checklist_zh.md` is the Chinese translation.

Confirm items one by one. A checked item states its **conclusion and evidence** (file, command output, measurement record or document); nothing is checked without evidence. If a result changes the interface or the design, update `docs/ABI.md` (and `docs/ABI_zh.md`) and `plan/RF_clock_modules.md` (and the Chinese version).

  - 4x2 side unknown at first: manual Figure 6 only shows "SMA → resistor divider → LMV7235, threshold from a 3.3 V divider" without values; to be measured on the board (resistance of the PPS SMA to ground, comparator output polarity)

  - The FS725 1PPS seen on a scope, about 30 µs wide; the 500 mV reading did not match the manual (5 V into high impedance), probe ratio/input impedance to be checked
  - Schematic (`RF-clock-PC:~/ref/docs/sch/rfsoc4x2_sch.pdf` page 10, 1PPS): J7 → 49.9 Ω to ground → 100 Ω → 10 nF AC coupling → node biased at 1.65 V → LMV7235 (about ±0.17 V hysteresis, output pulled up to 3.3 V through 1 kΩ) and SN74AUP1G17; an NVT2008 translates to 1.8 V with no pull-up on the 1.8 V side (the FPGA pull-up is needed).   - run4 on the board (2026-10-13): comp idle 0, trig idle 1, no edges on either, so no pulses at J7. To check: is the cable on J7 (net IRIG); measure the amplitude at the board with a tee (about 2.5 V into 50 Ω)
  - Conclusion: the PPS cable had not been on J7; connected, about 2.5 V at the board, one rising edge per second on both copies, idle 0 (positive pulses); the default `PPS_CONFIG` = 0 (comparator, not inverted) is used

- [x] **LMX2594 phase SYNC** (2026-10-19, rf-clock 1.3.0.4): without it the LMX channel divider started in an arbitrary phase at every clock write, the 480 MHz tile reference moved by whole VCO periods (130.2 ps), and after MTS the converters sat at positions of a 26.04 ps lattice (1/4 DAC sample, 1/8 ADC sample), unseen by MTS: on the scope the DAC took two positions 52 ps apart (first read as 260.4 ps steps; corrected by the M3 loopback, `results/lmx-sync-loopback-20261019/`). Found in the overnight restart test, traced to the clock writes (mixer bypassed; `--no-clocks` kept the state, clock writes changed it). The image now has VCO_PHASE_SYNC = 1 and PLL_N 40 (IncludedDivide 4, same VCO), and `program_clocks` toggles VCO_PHASE_SYNC after writing: SYNC category 2 of TI SNAS696C, software only, timing not critical. Verified: ten clock writes and ten reboots each left both boards at one start time (run-to-run sd 2.4-3.5 ps and 5-7 ps). Report: `results/lmx-phase-sync-20261019/`
  - [x] Both converters, with the wired M3 loopback (2026-10-19/20): with both LMX2594 synchronized the loopback time stayed within 0.10 ps (sd, carrier phase) over ten clock writes. Without the SYNC the DAC took three adjacent lattice positions over 80 clock writes (26.3 and 27.0 ps apart) and, with the clocks written as before 1.3.0.4 over 15 reboots, only the outer two, 52.5 ps apart: the scope's pair, reproduced. The ADC took two adjacent positions over clock writes and, the old way over reboots, three of the four 52 ps states once predicted. The step is one VCO period; the old writing after a reboot moves by pairs. The PYNQ device tree has the two LMX2594 names swapped (`lmxdac`, spi0.1, clocks the ADC; found by powering each down and reading the tile PLL locks); rf-clock writes both alike and is not affected. MTS latencies identical in all 251 runs. Report: `results/lmx-sync-loopback-20261019/`
  - [ ] **Each LMX2594's VCO band** (2026-10-20, 887329260072A): every clock write recalibrates the VCO, which picks one of two neighbouring capacitor bands (VCO_CAPCTRL; ADC chip 117/118, DAC chip 122/123), with or without a reboot. One band higher puts that converter 8-10 ps later (+9.6 ps ADC 117, +7.7 ps DAC 123); read back over SPI (`experiments/m3_vco_band.py`) and reproduced by forcing the band. The phase SYNC does not cover it. Explains the "9.7 ps reboot state" and the rare extra 7.6 ps at the low TX amplitude. rf-clock 1.3.0.6 (report section 5): a per-board config, `/etc/rf-clock/board.json` named by the board's AT24MAC402 serial number, sets each chip's band, which `PL.open()` forces, reads back and requires to lock; without it `PL.open()` warns and records the calibrated bands. `rf_clock.checks.vco_bands scan` suggests the band each chip's calibration picks most often and finds the bands that lock (on 887329260072A: ADC 109-118, DAC 113-123; the calibration picks one of the top two); `delays` measures each band's loopback delay into the config's band table (docs/ABI.md §11.1, §13). On 887329260072A (2026-10-20): within a band the loopback time held to 0.1 ps over 2.5 hours (die 42.1-43.8 °C) while the band changed in 31 of 120 clock writes; the config forces ADC 118 / DAC 122 (band table: ADC 117 +9.42 ps, 116 +17.48 ps; DAC 123 +7.76, 121 -9.71, 120 -17.62 ps; `results/vco-bands-20261020_214500/`), and PL.open with it passed M3. 887329260101: the calibration picked ADC 125 / DAC 128 in 20 of 20 writes, written to its config, but with its 10 MHz reference disconnected (tick 73 ppm slow, no PPS lock): scan again once it is back. Open: lock of the forced bands at other temperatures (ADC 118 is the highest band that locks); the band table of 887329260101 needs its own loopback cable
